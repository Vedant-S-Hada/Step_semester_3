About Futurix

Building a future-ready tech ecosystem on campus.

Mission

To create a student-led launchpad where technology meets leadership, creativity, and real-world execution.

Vision

To become the most recognized innovation community among engineering campuses for product outcomes and career impact.

Core Focus Areas

Structured growth across the full innovation lifecycle.

Build

Hands-on coding sprints, mini products, and engineering cohorts.

Compete

Hackathons and challenge tracks that reward speed, quality, and originality.

Network

Founder talks, alumni sessions, and mentor office hours.

Scale

Showcase day, internship pipelines, and startup-ready team formation.
